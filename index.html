OUR PURPOSE

More Than a Club.
A Green Movement.

At Sona College of Technology, Nature's Club turns ecological awareness into real campus action. We unite students to nurture green cover, manage our student terrace garden, map native trees, and build sustainable habits that last a lifetime.

🌱

Grow

Cultivating terrace crops, sowing seeds, and expanding green cover.

🌍

Protect

Mapping campus tree species, saving water, and reducing plastic.

🤝

Inspire

Hosting Green Heist quests and empowering student environmental leadership.

Sona Green Vanguard

"Every seed sown today on our terrace and campus grounds is a greener breath for tomorrow."
